# Independent review

The author of a change never reviews, approves or merges it. The reviewer has
a clean context: the ticket, the spec section and the diff, and nothing the
author said while working. A session that wrote the brief and then watched the
build counts as close to the author; prefer a fresh agent. At every risk
level, at least one reviewer is from a different provider than the author's,
because a model from the same family misses what the author missed.

**Applies when:** choosing a reviewer, recording a verdict, or standing at the
merge gate.
